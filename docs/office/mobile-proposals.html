The office, wherever the day takes you.

Three directions for the mobile and tablet app. Each keeps the familiar Fleming colours and Arial type, with large touch targets. These are interactive design proposals using example data.

Recommended foundation

Start with what needs doing.

A short daily agenda gives Sam the next useful action when he opens the app. The property record stays one tap away.

  • Today’s viewings, handovers and follow-ups, filtered to the signed-in person.
  • One prominent “Take inventory” action; choose the property and tenancy before taking photos.
  • Unsent changes and uploads have a visible status. A failed upload stays available to retry.
  • Four destinations: Today, Properties, Inbox and More. Flemo and feedback stay within reach.

Build this as the main app, with the inspection flow from direction 03. It gives the office a useful daily tool without making every screen a dashboard.

Best for visits and portfolio browsing

Everything starts at the property.

A focused property page connects the tenancy, documents, keys, issues and inspection history. Useful when the team visits several properties in one day.

  • Address, current tenancy and the next appointment at the top.
  • Quick actions for calling, directions, taking an inventory and reporting an issue.
  • Current tenants in notes; previous tenancies remain accessible in history.
  • A split view on tablet keeps the property list beside the selected record.

Trade-off: follow-ups across the whole business need a separate Today view. This works best as the property screen within the recommended app.

Recommended inspection flow

One room. Clear evidence.

Capture the inspection room by room. Large camera and note controls work while walking around a property.

  • Choose tenancy → add rooms → photograph and record condition → check the draft.
  • Show photo progress and missing information before issuing the inventory.
  • After issue, lock the office’s original. Each tenant gets an individual review link, can comment on each photo, add evidence and sign.
  • Desktop companion: upload a completed PDF and date, then review tenant responses from the property record.

The “no comments by the deadline” wording is shown to tenants. It must never fabricate a tenant signature. Offline capture and reliable queued uploads should be a separately tested app milestone before advertising offline support.
